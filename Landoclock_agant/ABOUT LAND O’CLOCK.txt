ABOUT LAND O’CLOCK
________________




Who We Are:


Land O’Clock is an innovative, AI-powered real estate platform transforming the way people rent, and manage properties. We connect tenants with verified landlords and trusted agents, ensuring speed, safety, and transparency in every transaction. Our mission is to eliminate the stress of house hunting and property management by offering instant property matching, fraud prevention, and seamless communication tools. We are committed to helping users find their ideal home and move in within 30 minutes, while empowering landlords to efficiently manage and grow their rental business. With ambitions to expand globally, Land O’Clock is building the future of real estate faster, smarter, and more reliable.


Problem We Are Solving:


In Rwanda, finding a new home or reliable tenant is often slow, costly, and stressful for both tenants and landlords. Tenants spend days or weeks searching, paying middlemen, and visiting unsuitable properties, while landlords struggle with late payments, property management across multiple locations, and conflicts with tenants. There is no centralized, secure, and transparent system to connect both parties efficiently. This leads to wasted time, increased costs, disputes, and lost opportunities for both sides.


Problem We Are Solving:


In Rwanda’s fast-growing real estate market, both tenants and landlords face significant inefficiencies. Tenants spend excessive time and money dealing with unverified listings, unreliable agents, and properties that don’t match their needs. Landlords face vacancy losses, delayed rent payments, and a lack of trusted property management solutions. The process is fragmented, largely offline, and vulnerable to fraud — resulting in wasted resources, lost revenue, and frustration for all stakeholders. There is an urgent need for a centralized, AI-powered, and transparent platform that connects verified tenants, landlords, and agents seamlessly, saving time, reducing costs, and building trust.


Our Solution:


Land O’Clock is an AI-powered real estate platform designed to eliminate the stress, cost, and uncertainty in finding or renting a property. We connect tenants directly with verified agents and landlords, ensuring trusted, transparent, and fraud-free transactions. Our smart property-matching algorithm recommends listings based on user preferences, saving time and reducing wasted visits. For landlords, we offer streamlined property management tools, tenant screening, and rent tracking to minimize vacancies and payment delays. By centralizing the process online, we make property rental and management faster, safer, and more accessible empowering everyone to make confident housing decisions.






Mission
 To revolutionize the way people find and secure homes by providing a fast, trusted, and AI-powered platform that enables users to discover, verify, and move into their ideal property in as little as 30 minutes anytime, anywhere.
Vision
 To become the world’s leading real estate technology platform, expanding into multiple countries and transforming property rental and management into a seamless, transparent, and instant experience for millions of people globally.
