1. GENERAL PLATFORM FAQ
1.1 What is Land O’Clock?
Land O’Clock is an AI-powered real estate platform that helps tenants find verified properties quickly, assists landlords in managing their properties digitally, and ensures fast, transparent, and secure rental transactions.
1.2 Who can use Land O’Clock?
* Tenants

* Landlords

* Property managers

* Real estate agents

* Investors

* Government institutions

1.3 How does Land O’Clock make renting faster?
The platform uses AI to instantly match users with properties that meet their preferences. Most tenants can find a home, talk to the landlord, and complete the process in under 1 hour.
1.4 Does Land O’Clock verify properties?
Yes. Every landlord and property goes through document verification and a structured quality check to prevent fraud.
