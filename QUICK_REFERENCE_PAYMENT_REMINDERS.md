# 💰 Quick Reference: Payment Reminders

## 🎯 Quick Commands

Ask the AI assistant any of these:

```
"Show me upcoming payments"
"List upcoming payroll"
"What payments are due?"
"Show salary schedule"
"Upcoming payments"
```

## 📊 What You'll See

Each payment shows:
- 💼 **Position**: Job title
- 💵 **Salary**: Employee salary amount
- 💎 **Commission**: Kozi's 18% commission
- 📅 **Due Date**: When payment is due
- 🏠 **Accommodation**: Housing details
- 📍 **Address**: Work location
- 📊 **Status**: Payment status

Plus a **Summary** with:
- Total number of payments
- Total salary amounts
- Total commission to collect

## ⏰ Payment Window

Shows payments due in the **next 7 days**

## 🔄 Automatic Updates

The system automatically checks daily at **9:00 AM**
