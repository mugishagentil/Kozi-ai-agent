Brand Overview
Kozi Rwanda (Kozi E-recruitment Ltd) is an e-recruitment / digital employment platform based in Kigali, Rwanda. It connects skilled local workers (support workers and professional workers) with employers (companies, households, and individuals).
Slogan / positioning:
* Empowering Local Talent. Supporting Rwandan Families.

* Kozi focuses on dignity through work, giving people a chance to earn, grow, and thrive.

* Kozi makes hiring easier, safer, and more transparent for both employers and workers.

Key numbers mentioned in public content:
   * 25+ Companies served

   * 125+ Individuals served

   * 5000+ Job seekers registered

   * 10+ Years of excellence (experience of leadership and/or team)

   * 320+ Job placements

   * 100% Local leadership

KB-2: Mission, Vision & Core Idea
Mission
         * To connect Rwandans to decent work through technology.

         * To empower both support workers and professionals with visibility, respect, and opportunity.

Vision
            * To become Africa’s leading inclusive job platform, where every worker — regardless of background — is valued, protected, and given a chance to grow.

Core Idea
               * Kozi was created around the belief that everyone deserves access to dignified work, and that “small” jobs (cleaning, cooking, driving, guarding, babysitting, etc.) are often the ones that feed families, pay school fees, and give second chances.

KB-3: Kozi Story & Timeline
Origin
                  * Kozi Rwanda started in 2021 as a small initiative helping families find trusted housemaids.

                  * Early operations ran as a WhatsApp-based connection service for housemaids.

                  * Kozi quickly realised a deeper problem: many skilled Rwandans had low visibility, limited support, and almost no voice in the job market.

Timeline
                     * 2022 – The Beginning

                        * Started as a WhatsApp connection service for housemaids.

                        * Placed 15+ workers into Kigali homes.

                        * Learned about the trust gap between workers and employers.

                           * 2023 – Building the Foundation

                              * Launched the first digital platform for job listings.

                              * Partnered with 3 local cleaning companies and a training center.

                              * Onboarded 120+ support workers.

                                 * 2025 – Platform with Purpose

                                    * Fully launched the Kozi mobile app.

                                    * Added employer dashboards, instant matching, and digital contracts.

                                    * Recognised as a social impact-driven tech platform in Rwanda.

Narrative theme:
                                       * Kozi grew from a simple matching idea into a platform with purpose, focusing on job dignity, legal protection, and equal opportunity.

KB-7: How Hiring Works on Kozi (High-Level Flow)
This section describes the typical employer journey on Kozi.
7.1 Step 1 – Post Your Request
                                                                                                                                          * Employer describes the type of worker needed:

                                                                                                                                             * Example: housemaid, cleaner, chef, babysitter, plumber, technician, driver, etc.

                                                                                                                                                * They provide key details:

                                                                                                                                                   * Location

                                                                                                                                                   * Job type: full-time, part-time, or one-time

                                                                                                                                                   * Preferred start date

                                                                                                                                                   * Additional requirements like gender, age, language preferences, etc.

On paper/offline, this is captured by the Job Provider Request Form, which includes fields for:
                                                                                                                                                      * Client name, address, phone

                                                                                                                                                      * Working hours

                                                                                                                                                      * Full-time / part-time / live-in

                                                                                                                                                      * Job title

                                                                                                                                                      * Starting date

                                                                                                                                                      * Gender and age preferences

                                                                                                                                                      * Job description & tasks

                                                                                                                                                      * Responsibilities and additional expectations

                                                                                                                                                      * Language skills

                                                                                                                                                      * Salary range (minimum 60,000 RWF)

                                                                                                                                                      * Agreement to pay one-time service fee of 30,000 RWF

7.2 Step 2 – Kozi Matches You
                                                                                                                                                         * Kozi’s system uses a database of pre-screened, trained workers.

                                                                                                                                                         * It matches based on:

                                                                                                                                                            * Availability

                                                                                                                                                            * Location

                                                                                                                                                            * Experience and skills

                                                                                                                                                            * Specific requirements (e.g., job type, gender, language)

7.3 Step 3 – Review, Connect & Hire
                                                                                                                                                               * Employer receives a shortlist of worker profiles.

                                                                                                                                                               * Can view:

                                                                                                                                                                  * Experience

                                                                                                                                                                  * Skills

                                                                                                                                                                  * Ratings (if available)

                                                                                                                                                                  * Contact or profile info

                                                                                                                                                                     * Employer chooses the best fit and can:

                                                                                                                                                                        * Contact them directly

                                                                                                                                                                        * Or request Kozi to help arrange the first meeting or trial

7.4 After Hiring
                                                                                                                                                                           * Worker is under Kozi’s care and legal contract.

                                                                                                                                                                           * Kozi:

                                                                                                                                                                              * Follows up on performance and wellbeing

                                                                                                                                                                              * Manages discipline if there are issues

                                                                                                                                                                              * Provides replacement at no extra cost if necessary

                                                                                                                                                                                 * Kozi promotes access to:

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Health insurance

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Fair, standardised salary

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Support in case of conflict

KB-10: Policies, Pricing & Contracts
Service Fee (Employer Side)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * The Job Provider Request Form includes a line:
“I agree to pay one time payment of 30,000 Frw as Service fees.”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * This suggests employers pay a single service fee when engaging Kozi to place a worker.

Salary Range
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Salary range field in the form indicates minimum 60,000 RWF for certain roles.

Contracting
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Workers are legally contracted under Kozi.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Kozi assumes responsibility for:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Follow-up

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Conduct and performance monitoring

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Replacement if needed, at no extra cost.

No Commission Middlemen
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Kozi is not a commission-based agency.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Kozi positions itself differently from informal brokers by taking full accountability rather than taking a cut and disappearing.

Data & Security
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Kozi must comply with NCSA security requirements and define what AI can or cannot access.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Data access, retention, and AI behaviour need to follow Kozi’s data access policies.

KB-14: Typical FAQs – Employers
These Q&A style chunks are ideal for embeddings.
Q: What is Kozi Rwanda?
 A: Kozi Rwanda is a digital employment platform that connects households, individuals, and companies with pre-screened, trained support workers and professional staff across Rwanda. It focuses on dignified, decent work and transparent hiring.
Q: How do I request a worker?
 A: You can fill out a request on the Kozi platform or via the Job Provider Request Form. Provide job title, job type (full-time, part-time, live-in, or one-time), location, start date, working hours, salary range (minimum 60,000 RWF for certain roles), and any preferences like gender, age, language, and tasks.
Q: What types of workers can I hire through Kozi?
 A: You can hire housemaids, cleaners, babysitters, cooks/chefs, drivers, movers, security guards, and professional workers like accountants, IT technicians, customer service agents, secretaries, and more.
Q: Are Kozi workers vetted?
 A: Yes. Kozi workers are selected, trained, and verified to ensure safety and reliability.
Q: How does payment work?
 A: Employers pay an agreed salary directly to the worker, following Kozi’s standardized salary guidelines. In addition, the employer pays Kozi a one-time service fee (e.g., 30,000 RWF) for recruitment services, as indicated in the Job Provider Request Form.
Q: What if the worker is not performing well?
 A: Kozi is responsible for follow-up and discipline. If a worker is not performing as expected, Kozi can replace them at no extra cost, as the worker is under Kozi’s care.
Q: Does Kozi provide short-term workers?
 A: Yes, Kozi supports one-time, part-time, and full-time jobs. Employers can request workers for regular support, occasional help, or one-off events.

KB-15: Typical FAQs – Job Seekers
Q: How can I register as a worker on Kozi?
 A: You can sign up on the Kozi platform or through Kozi’s registration process (online or in person, depending on current practice). You will provide personal details, experience, references, and preferred types of work.
Q: What kinds of jobs can I find on Kozi?
 A: Jobs include housemaid roles, cleaning, babysitting, driving, cooking, security work, as well as professional roles like accounting, IT support, customer service, reception, and more.
Q: Does Kozi help me get a contract?
 A: Yes. Kozi provides legal contracts for workers hired through the platform, ensuring clear terms and protection.
Q: Can I get health insurance through Kozi?
 A: Kozi aims to connect workers with jobs that include health insurance, reflecting its value of protecting workers’ wellbeing.
Q: How are salaries decided?
 A: Kozi has standardized salary ranges to ensure fair, transparent pay. Salaries are discussed with you before placement, and you know what you will earn.
Q: What if there is a problem with my employer?
 A: Because Kozi is accountable (not a simple broker), the team can help mediate issues and, when necessary, reassign you to another employer.
