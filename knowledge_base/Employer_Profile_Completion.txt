Employer Profile Completion – Step by Step
________________
Open the Profile Page
“Please look at the left sidebar menu on your screen.
At the top of the sidebar, click on ‘Edit Profile’.”
Step 1: Account Setup
Create an employer account by choosing your account type:
* Individual / Household

* Company

* Organization

* Institution

This tells Kozi what kind of employer you are.
________________


Step 2: Personal / Business Information
Fill in the basic identity fields:
   * First Name

   * Last Name

   * Country

   * Telephone Number

   * Gender (optional)

✅ Purpose: Identity verification and secure communication.
________________


Step 3: Location Details
Select your full address from the dropdowns:
      * Province

      * District

      * Sector

      * Cell

      * Village

✅ Purpose: Helps Kozi match you with nearby workers.
________________


Step 4: Upload Profile Image
Upload a profile image (or company logo).
Rules:
         * Max size: 1MB

         * Clear image

         * JPG/PNG format

✅ This builds trust with workers.
________________


Step 5: Complete Employer Profile Description (Optional but Recommended)
Write a short professional description such as:
            * Type of work environment (home/business)

            * Values (respect, fairness)

            * Working style

Example:
“We are a respectful household looking for responsible and honest workers.”
________________


Step 6: Verify Phone Number
You will receive a code by:
               * SMS or

               * WhatsApp

Enter this code to verify your account.
________________


Step 7: Save and Activate Profile
Click Save Profile.
Once saved:
✅ You can now post jobs
✅ View worker profiles
✅ Use Kozi services
________________


Step 8: Post Your First Job (After Profile Completion)
Use the official job form to request workers:
📄 Local file reference (as required for your system):
/mnt/data/Job Providers Request Form.pdf
This form collects:
                  * Job role

                  * Worker type

                  * Salary range

                  * Working hours

                  * Special requirements

________________

KOZI — EMPLOYER FAQ
________________


Employer Account & Profile
Q: Who is an Employer on Kozi?
 A: An Employer is an individual, household, company, or organization that uses Kozi to find and hire workers.
________________


Q: How do I create an Employer account?
 A: Register on the Kozi platform and choose your account type (Individual, Company, Organization, or Institution).
________________


Q: How do I complete my Employer profile?
 A: Click “Edit Profile” from the left sidebar, then fill in your personal/business details, location, and upload a profile image.
________________


Q: Can I edit my Employer profile later?
 A: Yes, you can update your profile information at any time.
________________


Q: Why is my phone number required?
 A: Your phone number is used for account verification and communication with workers.
________________


Q: Is my personal information safe?
 A: Yes. Kozi protects your information and follows strict data protection laws.
