How_Employers_Post_Job
________________


Purpose
This guide helps employers understand how to correctly post a job on the Kozi platform so they can quickly receive qualified worker applications.
________________


Step 1: Open the Job Posting Page
AI should instruct the user:
“On the left sidebar, click on ‘Add Job’ to open the job posting form.”
________________


Step 2: Fill in Job Title
Field: Job Title
What to add:
* Clear job name
Examples:

* Cleaner

* Housemaid

* Driver

* Office Assistant

* IT Technician

Why it matters:
   * Helps workers understand the role

   * Improves search visibility

________________


Step 3: Fill Company Name / Your Name
Field: Company Name / Your Name
What to add:
      * If individual → Your full name

      * If business → Registered business name

Purpose:
         * Identifies who is hiring

         * Builds trust with applicants

________________


Step 4: Select Working Mode
Field: Working Mode
Options may include:
            * Full-time

            * Part-time

            * Contract

            * One-time job

Purpose:
               * Helps workers know the job schedule

________________


Step 5: Select Deadline Date
Field: Deadline Date
Format: DD/MM/YYYY
Example format:
                  * 30/12/2025

Purpose:
                     * Tells workers when applications close

________________


Step 6: Select Job Category
Field: Category
Examples:
                        * Basic Workers

                        * Professional Workers

                        * Technical Services

Purpose:
                           * Organizes jobs

                           * Improves matching accuracy

________________


Step 7: Upload Logo (If Applicable)
Field: Logo (optional)
Rules:
                              * Maximum file size: 1MB

                              * Accepted formats: JPG, PNG

Who should upload:
                                 * Companies or organizations may upload their logo

                                 * Individuals can skip this field

________________


Step 8: Write Job Description
Field: Job Description
What to include:
                                    * Summary of job

                                    * Work environment

                                    * Type of tasks

Example structure:
“This role involves maintaining cleanliness and hygiene in the workplace and supporting daily operations.”
________________


Step 9: Add Job Requirements
Field: Requirements
What to include:
                                       * Experience level

                                       * Education level (if needed)

                                       * Special skills (if any)

Examples:
                                          * Previous experience preferred

                                          * Good communication skills

                                          * Basic reading and writing

________________


Step 10: Add Job Responsibilities
Field: Responsibility
What to include:
                                             * Daily tasks

                                             * Main duties

Examples:
                                                * Cleaning rooms

                                                * Preparing meals

                                                * Supervising children

                                                * Driving safely

________________


Step 11: Set Salary Range
Fields:
                                                   * Salary Min

                                                   * Salary Max

What to enter:
                                                      * Minimum amount offered

                                                      * Maximum amount offered

Importance:
                                                         * Helps workers know expected pay

                                                         * Reduces salary conflicts

________________


Step 12: Write Conclusion
Field: Conclusion
What to write:
                                                            * A short closing message for applicants

Examples:
                                                               * “Only serious candidates should apply.”

                                                               * “We look forward to working with responsible applicants.”

________________


Step 13: Submit the Job
Final action:
Click Post Job or Submit button to publish the job.
Once submitted:
✅ Workers can view the job
✅ Applications can start arriving
KOZI – EMPLOYER “POST A JOB” FAQ
________________


Q: How do I start posting a job on Kozi?
A: Click “Add Job” on the left sidebar. This opens the job posting form where you can enter job details.
________________


Q: What should I write in the “Job Title” field?
A: Write a clear job role such as Cleaner, Driver, Housemaid, Office Assistant, Technician, or Babysitter. This helps workers understand the position.
________________


Q: What do I put in “Company Name / Your Name”?
A: If you are an individual, enter your full name.
If you are a business, enter your registered company name.
________________


Q: What is “Working Mode” and what should I choose?
A: Working Mode describes the type of work schedule. You can choose:
                                                                  * Full-time

                                                                  * Part-time

                                                                  * Contract

                                                                  * One-time job

________________


Q: Why do I need to set a Deadline Date?
A: The deadline date tells workers the last day they can apply for the job. It must be entered in DD/MM/YYYY format.
________________


Q: Why do I need to select a Category?
A: The category helps the system organize jobs and show your post to the right type of workers.
________________


Q: Is the logo upload required?
A: No. The logo is optional.
Businesses and organizations can upload their logo.
Maximum allowed file size is 1MB.
________________


Q: What should I write in the Job Description?
A: Write a short explanation of the job, work environment, and general tasks the worker will perform.
________________


Q: What is the difference between Requirements and Responsibilities?
A:
                                                                     * Requirements = Skills or experience needed for the job

                                                                     * Responsibilities = Daily tasks the worker will do

________________


Q: Why do I need to add Salary Minimum and Maximum?
A: Adding salary ranges helps workers understand the expected pay and reduces misunderstandings during hiring.
________________


Q: What should I write in the Conclusion field?
A: Write a short closing message such as:
                                                                        * “Only serious applicants should apply.”

                                                                        * “We look forward to working with responsible candidates.”

________________


Q: How do I finish posting the job?
A: After filling all fields, click the Post Job or Submit button. Your job will then be visible to workers.
________________


Q: What happens after I post a job?
A: Workers can start applying, and you will receive applications through the platform.
________________


Q: Can I edit a job after posting it?
A: Yes. You can edit or update your job post from your dashboard.
