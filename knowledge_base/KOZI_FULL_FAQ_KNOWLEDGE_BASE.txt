SECTION 1: GENERAL PLATFORM
Q: What is Kozi Rwanda?
 A: Kozi Rwanda is a digital employment and workforce management platform operated by Kozi E-Recruitment Ltd. It connects job seekers (domestic and professional workers) with employers (households, companies, institutions) in a safe, legal, and transparent way. Kozi focuses on dignity of work, worker protection, employer safety, and fair recruitment.
________________


Q: What makes Kozi different from brokers or agents?
 A: Kozi is not a casual middleman. It provides:
* Legal contracts

* Worker vetting

* Follow-ups and job monitoring

* Replacement guarantees

* Data protection
Unlike informal brokers, Kozi takes accountability for the recruitment process.
