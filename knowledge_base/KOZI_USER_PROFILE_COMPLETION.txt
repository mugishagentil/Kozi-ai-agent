KOZI USER PROFILE COMPLETION 
________________


KB-UP-1: Profile Completion — Purpose
Every user must complete their profile before applying for jobs. A complete profile helps:
* Match users with the right jobs

* Improve trust with employers

* Enable legal verification

* Improve AI job recommendations

Profiles must contain accurate and honest details.


________________


When the user asks how to complete their profile, the AI should respond like this:
Open the Profile Page
“Please look at the left sidebar menu on your screen.
At the top of the sidebar, click on ‘Edit Profile’.”


________________


KB-UP-2: Personal Information Fields
1. First Name
   * Should match the official name on the user’s ID.

2. Last Name
      * Used for legal and professional identification.

3. Father’s Name
         * Used for background verification and identity support.

4. Mother’s Name
            * Helps strengthen identity validation records.

5. Date of Birth
               * Format: DD/MM/YYYY

               * Used to confirm legal work eligibility and age compliance.

6. Telephone Number
                  * Must be active and reachable.

                  * Used for job notifications, security alerts, and employer communication.

7. Gender
                     * Used for legal and job-matching purposes only.

________________


KB-UP-3: Location Information
Users must fill their location details to help the system suggest nearby job opportunities.
Required fields:
                        * Province

                        * District

                        * Sector

                        * Cell

                        * Village

Accurate location data helps reduce transportation barriers and improves job matching.
________________


KB-UP-4: Disability Information

KOZI KB — AI GUIDE FOR PROFESSIONAL CV WRITING
________________


KB-CV-1: Purpose of a CV
A CV helps employers quickly understand:
                                                         * Skills

                                                         * Experience

                                                         * Work history

                                                         * Professional behavior

Even workers without formal education should create a simple CV.
________________


KB-CV-2: Standard CV Structure
1. Personal Information Section
Should contain:
                                                            * Full name

                                                            * Phone number

                                                            * District or city (general, not detailed address)

                                                            * Job title

________________


2. Professional Summary
A short paragraph (3–4 lines) describing:
                                                               * Years of experience

                                                               * Main skills

                                                               * Work attitude

AI Coaching Rule:
 Ask the user to describe their experience in simple words, then rewrite it professionally.
________________


3. Work Experience Section
Users should list:
                                                                  * Job title

                                                                  * Work place

                                                                  * Time period

                                                                  * Key responsibilities

Informal work is valid experience.
________________


4. Skills Section
Two types of skills:
Hard skills:
                                                                     * Technical or practical skills

Soft skills:
                                                                        * Personal and communication skills

________________


5. Education Section
List:
                                                                           * School name (optional)

                                                                           * Level achieved

                                                                           * Certificates or training if any

________________


6. Languages Section
Users should list languages and level:
                                                                              * Basic

                                                                              * Intermediate

                                                                              * Fluent

________________


7. References Section
Users should include:
                                                                                 * Name of referee

                                                                                 * Relationship

                                                                                 * Contact (with permission)

________________


KB-CV-3: AI Step-by-Step Coaching Flow
When a user requests help:
                                                                                    1. Ask what job they want

                                                                                    2. Ask about experience

                                                                                    3. Ask about skills

                                                                                    4. Ask about education

                                                                                    5. Ask about languages

Then generate the CV in simple, clean language.
________________


KB-CV-4: AI Safety Rules
AI must never:
                                                                                       * Encourage lying

                                                                                       * Create fake experience

                                                                                       * Invent fake references

                                                                                       * Ask for unnecessary private data

________________


KB-CV-5: Tone of the AI
The AI should always be:
                                                                                          * Encouraging

                                                                                          * Respectful

                                                                                          * Clear

                                                                                          * Simple

                                                                                          * Non-judgmental

JOB SEEKER PROFILE & CV FAQ
________________


Job Seeker Profile Completion – FAQ
Q: Why do I need to complete my job seeker profile?
 A: Completing your profile helps Kozi match you with the right jobs and helps employers trust your identity and skills.
________________


Q: What information is required to complete my profile?
 A: You must provide:
                                                                                             * First name and last name

                                                                                             * Parents’ names

                                                                                             * Date of birth

                                                                                             * Phone number

                                                                                             * Gender

                                                                                             * Full location (Province, District, Sector, Cell, Village)

                                                                                             * Work category

                                                                                             * Skills and experience

________________


Q: Why do I need to upload my ID card?
 A: Your ID helps Kozi verify your identity and protect employers and workers from fraud.
________________


Q: Why should I upload a profile photo?
 A: A clear photo helps employers trust your profile and easily recognize you.
________________


Q: What should I write in “Skills and Capabilities”?
 A: You should list what you know how to do, such as cleaning, cooking, driving, childcare, repairs, and soft skills like honesty and time management.
________________


Q: Can I edit my job seeker profile later?
 A: Yes. You can update your information at any time from your dashboard.
________________


Q: What happens if I leave my profile incomplete?
 A: You may not receive job matches or be approved for applications.
________________


Q: Is my profile visible to employers?
 A: Only safe and necessary information is shown to employers. Your private data is protected.
________________


Job Seeker CV Creation – FAQ
Q: What is a CV?
 A: A CV is a document that shows your skills, experience, and work history so employers can understand you better.
________________


Q: Do I need a CV to get a job?
 A: A CV is not mandatory, but it greatly increases your chances of getting hired.
________________


Q: Can beginners create a CV without experience?
 A: Yes. Even informal work like helping families, neighbors, or small businesses counts as experience.
________________


Q: What should be included in my CV?
 A: Your CV should include:
                                                                                                * Your full name

                                                                                                * Phone number

                                                                                                * Location (general)

                                                                                                * Job title

                                                                                                * Skills

                                                                                                * Work experience

                                                                                                * Education

                                                                                                * Languages

                                                                                                * References

________________


Q: How should I write my professional summary?
 A: Write 2–4 short sentences about:
                                                                                                   * Your main skills

                                                                                                   * Your experience

                                                                                                   * Your work attitude

________________


Q: How do I list my work experience?
 A: Include:
                                                                                                      * Job title

                                                                                                      * Place of work

                                                                                                      * Years worked

                                                                                                      * Main responsibilities

________________


Q: What are soft skills and should I include them?
 A: Soft skills are personal qualities like honesty, teamwork, and communication. Yes, they make your CV stronger.
________________


Q: Can Kozi help me write my CV?
 A: Yes. Kozi’s AI assistant can help you step by step.
________________


Q: What mistakes should I avoid in my CV?
 A: Avoid:
                                                                                                         * Lying about experience

                                                                                                         * Using rude words

                                                                                                         * Fake references

                                                                                                         * Poor spelling

________________


Q: Can I update my CV later?
 A: Yes. You can improve and update your CV at any time.
________________


Job Seeker Account & Profile Safety – FAQ
Q: Is my personal data safe?
 A: Yes. Kozi protects your data and follows strict data protection laws.
________________


Q: Can I delete my account?
 A: Yes. You can request account deletion by contacting support.
________________


Q: Who can see my uploaded documents?
 A: Only Kozi verification staff and approved systems can access your documents.
