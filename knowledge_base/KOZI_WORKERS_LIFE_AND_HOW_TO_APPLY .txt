KOZI KB – WORKERS LIFE & COMMUNITY
KB-WL-1: Workers Life – Overview
Section Title: The Kozi Worker Life
Kozi Rwanda is more than a recruitment platform. It is a worker empowerment movement focused on:
* Growth

* Healing

* Dignity

* Purpose

* Community

Kozi doesn’t just connect workers to jobs — it helps them grow, heal, and thrive.
Core message:
“More than a job, it’s a journey of growth, dignity, and purpose.”
________________


KB-WL-2: Kozi Worker Support Areas
Kozi provides structured development in five main areas:
1. Campus Life
   * Community-based learning

   * Worker peer support

   * Training environment that builds dignity and belonging

2. Professional Growth
      * Practical life and work skills training

      * Hygiene, food handling, etiquette, household management

      * Confidence-building

3. Job Replacement & Follow-Up
Kozi:
         * Places workers carefully

         * Monitors work progress

         * Provides consistent follow-ups

         * Ensures workers are in the right job fit

4. Saving Culture
Kozi trains workers to:
            * Save money

            * Build discipline

            * Gain financial independence

            * Plan long-term stability

5. Mental Wellness
Kozi supports mental health by:
               * Youth circles

               * Mentorship groups

               * Emotional support sessions

               * Community bonding

KOZI KB – HOW TO APPLY / JOB PROCESS
KB-HTA-1: Application Overview
Process goal:
Connect job seekers to verified employers through a secure and simple process.
Core principles:
                                 * One-click applications

                                 * Verified employers

                                 * Digital contracts

                                 * Continuous Kozi follow-up

________________


KB-HTA-2: Step-by-Step Job Application Flow
Step 1: Create an Account
User signs up as:
                                    * Individual (worker)

                                    * Company (employer)

Required details:
                                       * Name

                                       * Contact information

                                       * Job type interest (for workers or employers)

________________


Step 2: Find Worker or Post a Job
For Employers:
                                          * Describe role clearly:

                                             * Cleaner

                                             * Nanny

                                             * Receptionist

                                             * Chef

                                             * Technician

                                                * Include:

                                                   * Job Type: Full-time, Part-time, Gig

                                                   * Location

                                                   * Working Hours

________________


Step 3: Smart Matching System
Kozi automatically connects:
                                                      * Qualified workers

                                                      * Based on skills and experience

                                                      * Based on location

                                                      * Based on availability

Each worker profile shows:
                                                         * Work history

                                                         * Skills

                                                         * Experience

                                                         * Ratings / Reviews (when available)

________________


Step 4: Employer Response
Job seeker:
                                                            * Receives call or platform message

                                                            * May be invited for:

                                                               * Interview

                                                               * Trial shift

Kozi may assist in:
                                                                  * Scheduling follow-up

                                                                  * Coordinating communication

________________


Step 5: Contract & Onboarding
Kozi handles:
                                                                     * Digital contract creation

                                                                     * Onboarding

                                                                     * Job start coordination

Kozi continues to:
                                                                        * Monitor job experience

                                                                        * Follow up on satisfaction for both sides

________________
